# Brand — El Bingo del Cargador

> Brand rules for copy, UI, and content. Tone details live in
> `docs/frontend/COPYWRITING.md`; the visual system lives in
> `docs/frontend/DESIGN.md`.

## Voice & tone

Dry-sarcastic Spanish ("humor seco") about the real, everyday failures of
Spanish public EV charging. The laugh comes from recognition, not cruelty.

- **Do:** understate, let the situation be the joke, plain es-ES.
- **Don't:** edgy, insult players, punch at individuals, exaggerate into nonsense.

## Identity

- **Name:** "El Bingo del Cargador". Always complete in user-facing copy; the
  repo/package name (`ev-bingo`, `bingo-ev`) is internal only.
- **Site:** bingo.gruxon.com (parody, non-commercial).
- **Visuals:** palette, typography, and card geometry are owned by
  `docs/frontend/DESIGN.md` — do not restate them here.

## Principles

1. **No brand names anywhere** — no charger networks, no car makers, in any
   situation or copy.
2. **Honest limitations** — the product never hides what it can't do (see the
   guide's hard rules).
3. **UI strings are Spanish (es-ES), always**; code and comments are English.
4. **Non-commercial** — no ads, no upsell, no dark patterns.
